Processor (CPU) - The Brain. Don't get cheated by “i7” label

✓ Buy (2025-26)

Intel 13th/14th gen H/HS (13420H, 1355U), Ryzen 7000 (7530U, 7730U), Apple M2/M3. H = High performance.

✕ Avoid

11th gen or older, Intel N-series (N3050), Celeron, Pentium - slow & no resale.

Fresher rule

i5 13th gen > i7 11th gen. Always check generation first, not i3/i5/i7.

Coding → Ryzen 7 / i5-H / M2 General → Ryzen 5 7530U king under 40k MBA → Ultra 5 / Ryzen 5 (efficiency)

RAM - 8GB vs 16GB: The 4-year decision

8GB - Only if budget <40k and light use

OK for Docs/Zoom/YouTube. Stutters with 12 Chrome tabs + Excel + Meet.

16GB - Sweet spot for 95% students ✓

Future-proof for 4 years. Needed for coding (Docker, Android Studio), 30 tabs.

Always 512GB NVMe SSD. No HDD. 10× faster boot (<10 sec). 256GB fills in 1 sem.

Freshers Ask - We Answer

MacBook vs Windows for CSE?

If budget 80k+ and you can stretch: MacBook Air M2/M3 16GB is most peace-of-mind (15h, no heating, Unix). But Windows Ryzen 7 + 16GB + WSL2 does 95% same for 50-60k. Don't take loan for Mac in 1st year.

Is 8GB enough for coding?

1st sem yes, 3rd sem no. Android Studio + Chrome + Docker eats 12GB. 8GB soldered slows in 1 year. 16GB is real minimum for CSE.

Should I buy now or wait for sale?

If college starts July/Aug, buy in Great Indian Festival (Sept-Oct) or BBD - save 8-12k. Offline stores price-match if you show online price.

Extended warranty - worth it?

Yes for ₹2-3.5k. One motherboard failure = ₹25k. Get 2-yr + ADP if you carry daily. Register online.

Refurbished / second hand?

Avoid as first laptop - battery degraded, no warranty. Only brand-certified refurbished with 1yr warranty if budget <30k.

Intel vs AMD vs Apple?

2026 India: Ryzen 7000 = best efficiency + value. Intel H-series = raw power but hotter. Apple M2/M3 = best battery & build. For 40-60k, Ryzen 7 is king.
